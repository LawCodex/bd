# <sup>1</sup>The Districts Act, 1836

(ACT NO. XXI OF 1836)

#### Power to create new Districts

It shall be lawful for the <sup>2</sup>[\*\*\*] Government, by notification in the official Gazette, to create new districts in any part of Bangladesh.

<sup>3</sup>[Government may, from time to time, alter the limits of the existing districts in any part of Bangladesh].
