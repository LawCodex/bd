# THE CHITTAGONG HILL-TRACT LAWS REGULATIONS, 1943

(BENGAL REGULATION I OF 1943)

**A Regulation to apply the Explosive Substances Act, 1908, to the Chittagong Hill-tracts.**

WHEREAS it is expedient to apply the Explosive Substances Act, 1908, to the Chittagong Hill-tracts;

It is hereby enacted as follows:-

#### 1. Short title

1. This Regulation may be called the Chittagong Hill-tracts Laws Regulation, 1943.

#### 2. Application of Act VI of 1908 to the Chittagong Hill- tracts

2. The Explosive Substances Act, 1908, shall apply to the Chittagong Hill-tracts.
