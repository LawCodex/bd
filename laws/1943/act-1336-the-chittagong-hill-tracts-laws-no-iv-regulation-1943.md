# THE CHITTAGONG HILL-TRACTS LAWS (No. IV) REGULATION, 1943

(BENGAL REGULATION IV OF 1943)

**A Regulation to apply the <sup>1</sup>[\*\*\*] Ferries Act, 1885, to the Chittagong Hill-tracts.**

WHEREAS it is expedient to apply the <sup>2</sup>[\*\*\*] Ferries Act, 1885, to the Chittagong Hill-tracts;

It is hereby enacted as follows:-

#### 1. Short title

1. This Regulation may be called THE CHITTAGONG HILL-TRACTS LAWS (No. IV) REGULATION, 1943.

#### 2. Application of Bengal Act I of 1885 to the Chittagong Hill- tracts

2. The <sup>3</sup>[\*\*\*] Ferries Act, 1885, with the exception of section 3 of the said Act, shall apply to the Chittagong Hill-tracts.
