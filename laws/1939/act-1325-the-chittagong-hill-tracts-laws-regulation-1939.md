# THE CHITTAGONG HILL-TRACTS LAWS REGULATION, 1939

(BENGAL REGULATION iii OF 1939)

**A Regulation to apply the 1[\*\*\*] Public Gambling Act, 1867, to the Chittagong Hill-tracts.**

WHEREAS it is expedient to apply the 1[\*\*\*] Public Gambling Act, 1867, to the whole of the Chittagong Hill- tracts;

It is hereby enacted as follows:-

#### Short title

**1.**This Regulation may be called the Chittagong Hill- tracts Laws Regulation, 1939.

#### Applications of Bengal Act II of 1867 to the Chittagong Hill- tracts

**2.**The <sup>1</sup>[\*\*\*] Public Gambling Act, 1867, shall apply to the whole of the Chittagong Hill-tracts.
