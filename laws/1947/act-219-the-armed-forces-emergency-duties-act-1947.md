# The Armed Forces (Emergency Duties) Act, 1947

(ACT NO. XV OF 1947)

**An Act to enable duties in connection with vital services to be imposed in an emergency on the Armed Forces of Bangladesh.<sup>1</sup>♣**

WHEREAS it is expedient to enable duties in connection with vital services to be imposed in an emergency on the Armed Forces of Bangladesh;

It is hereby enacted as follows:-

#### 1. Short title and extent

1. (1) This Act may be called the Armed Forces (Emergency Duties) Act, 1947.

(2) It extends to the whole of Bangladesh.

#### 2. Emergency duties of Armed Forces

2. (1) The <sup>2</sup>[Government] may, by notification in the official Gazette, declare any specified service to be a service of vital importance to the community:

Provided that such notification shall remain in force for one month in the first instance, but may be extended, from time to time, by a like notification.

(2) Upon a declaration being made under sub-section (1) and until it is rescinded, it shall be the duty of every person subject to the <sup>3</sup>[Army Act, 1952, or the Air Force Act, 1953, or the Navy Ordinance, 1961], to obey any command given by any superior officer in relation to employment upon or in connection with the service specified in the declaration; and every such command shall be deemed to be a lawful command within the meaning and for the purposes of the said Acts or Ordinance.

#### 3. Repealed

3. [Validation of certain past commands.- Repealed by section 3 and Second Schedule of the Federal Laws (Revision and Declaration) Act, 1951 (Act No. XXVI of 1951).]
